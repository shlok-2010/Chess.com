CrowdSolve

AI-Powered Community Issue Reporting & Resolution Platform

1. Executive Summary

CrowdSolve is a community-driven web platform that enables citizens to report local issues such as potholes, broken streetlights, water leakage, garbage accumulation, public safety concerns, and infrastructure problems.

The platform leverages Google's Gemini AI to automatically categorize issues, detect duplicates, assign priority levels, and summarize complaints. Local communities can support issues through voting and comments, helping authorities identify and resolve the most impactful problems.

The goal is to create a transparent, collaborative, and intelligent civic engagement platform.

2. Problem Statement

  • Citizens struggle to report civic issues efficiently.
  • Duplicate complaints create noise.
  • Authorities lack prioritization mechanisms.
  • Citizens cannot easily track issue progress.
  • Many issues remain unresolved due to lack of visibility.

3. Proposed Solution

CrowdSolve provides a centralized platform where users can:

  • Report issues with images and location.
  • View issues on an interactive map.
  • Vote on issues requiring attention.
  • Receive real-time updates.
  • Track issue resolution progress.
  • Use AI to streamline issue management.

4. Objectives

  • Increase citizen participation.
  • Improve transparency.
  • Reduce duplicate complaints.
  • Enable faster issue prioritization.
  • Create data-driven civic management.

8. User Workflow

  1. User registers and logs in.
  2. User reports an issue.
  3. Image and location are uploaded.
  4. Gemini analyzes the complaint.
  5. Issue appears on map.
  6. Community votes and comments.
  7. Authorities update status.
  8. User receives notifications.
  9. Issue gets resolved.
